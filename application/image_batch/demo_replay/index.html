Policy comparison

Three policies, one workload.

Static

Keep processing capacity fixed as demand changes. The operator sets capacity to cover expected peak demand.

Strength
Simple and predictable.
Control
Fixed processing capacity.
Tradeoff
Must cover peak demand.

Heuristic

Use observed demand and threshold rules to adjust capacity. Scale up or down after the load changes.

Strength
Adapts to observed demand.
Control
Reactive threshold rules.
Tradeoff
Acts after demand changes.

Digital twin

Forecast demand and simulate capacity options. Choose an action, apply it after safety checks, then observe the response.

Strength
Anticipates future demand.
Control
Simulate, choose, apply.
Tradeoff
Depends on model quality.

What the digital twin adds

All three policies can monitor current state. The twin adds forecasts and simulated alternatives before choosing an action, then checks the measured physical response.

Recorded settings

All three policies can use monitoring. The heuristic policy reacts to observed demand using threshold rules; the digital twin also forecasts demand and simulates alternatives before choosing an action. The recorded heuristic is a worker-capacity policy, not Kubernetes HPA or a benchmark of every reactive autoscaler.

Capacity allocation is not measured energy use or a financial saving. Reserve VMs remain powered on.
